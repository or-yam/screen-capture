# Screen Capture

Terms for the region-capture and annotation workflow.

## Language

**Capture session**:
One capture workflow, from opening region selection until saving, copying, or exiting.

**Capture display**:
The single screen chosen by the initial capture selection for the rest of that capture session.
_Avoid_: active screen, current screen (the pointer or keyboard focus can change without changing the capture display)

**Capture frame**:
The selected rectangle whose contents will be copied or saved, within the capture display.
